How to read

The rail is the plan in source order — every declared stage is a node, with or without tasks. Each node keeps three facts separate: the declared status the plan records, the local task counts from the ledger, and the recorded exit-criteria evidence. Dated spans are observed local records only; a stage with none says so instead of guessing. Click a node to open its criteria, evidence and tasks.
